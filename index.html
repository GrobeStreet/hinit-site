HIN It

A free boat VIN (HIN) check. Decode a hull number on your phone and jump straight to the official free sources — no fees, no account.

What it does

HIN It reads a boat's 12-character Hull Identification Number — the builder's code, the month and year it was built, and the model year — right on your device. It also checks the number for a mistyped or altered hull ID, and links you to the official, free government sources for recalls, documented-vessel records and your state's registration and title records.

It does not provide ownership, accident, title or theft history — no free source does for boats. HIN It tells you plainly where to check instead.

Where to find a HIN

Look at the upper-right corner of the transom (the flat back of the boat), on the starboard (right) side. A second, hidden copy is required elsewhere on the hull.

Privacy

HIN It collects no data. There is no account and no sign-in. Everything you type is decoded on your device and is not sent anywhere. The app has no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising SDKs. Recently checked hull numbers are stored only on your device and can be cleared in the app.

The "Verify with official free sources" buttons open public government web pages (US Coast Guard, your state boating agency) in your browser; from that point those sites' own privacy policies apply.

HIN It is not affiliated with the US Coast Guard, the NICB, or any boat-history report seller.
